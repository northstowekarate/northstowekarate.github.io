# Kyu Grading — 5 Dec 2026

Kyu grading hosted by [Cambridge Karate Dojo](https://cambridgekaratedojo.com). Grading will consist of a 1-hour training session with Sensei Roy Tomlin, 7th Dan to help prepare for your grading, followed by grading assessment.

Belts and certificates will be awarded after the grading, during regularly training sessions.

## Readiness

* Wear your dogi (uniform)
* Have active membership/license - please check your license is in date, if it's expired, you must reapply before the grading
  * If you have applied and not yet received your license, you will still be able to grade
* Have confirmation from the club instructor you are ready - readiness assessment will be done 1-3 weeks before the grading.


## Fees

The grading fee is £25 - this includes the training fee - you must attend the training session if you want to grade. 

The training fee is £15 for those who want to only train.

## What to bring

* Your uniform (belt and dogi)
* Your license
* Water bottle

## Event Details

* 1-2pm - training for purple belt and above
* 2-3pm - training for green belt and below
* 3-4pm - grading for white, orange and red belts
* 4-6pm - grading for yellow belts and above

You must attend one of the training sessions if you want to grade.

Please note grading times are approximate and depend on numbers.

Please arrive 15 minutes early before your session starts. 

Parents are welcome to watch quietly from the side.

### Location

University of Cambridge Sports Centre. Please park in [Car Park B](https://www.sport.cam.ac.uk/about-us/plan-your-visit/university-cambridge-sports-centre).
